Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Todor Ilinov ("TI Apps", "we", "I") handles information in the mobile applications published under this developer account.

TV DSP Center — Equalizer (com.tvdsp.center)

World Radio (com.worldradio.app)

Fake Location (com.fakelocation.app)

Information we do not collect

Our apps do not require you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or any other personally identifying information, and none of our apps send your personal data to us. Audio processed by TV DSP Center never leaves your device. Fake Location reads your device's location, but only on the device itself — see the section on Fake Location below.

Information collected automatically

The apps use the following third-party services, which may collect limited technical data:

  • Google Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage statistics (screens viewed, session length, device model, country, app version). Used to understand how the apps are used and to improve them.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports (device model, OS version, stack traces). Used to find and fix bugs.
  • Google AdMob — serves advertisements. AdMob may use an advertising identifier to show relevant ads. See Google's Advertising Policy.

This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. It is processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

TV DSP Center offers an optional one-time in-app purchase (Pro upgrade). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Internet radio streams

World Radio streams stations provided by publicly available directories (such as the Radio Browser project). When you play a station, your device connects directly to that broadcaster's stream server, which may log standard connection data (such as your IP address) under the broadcaster's own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Audio settings / modify audio (TV DSP Center) — required to apply equalizer and DSP effects.
  • Internet — required for radio streaming, ads and analytics.
  • Foreground service / notifications (World Radio) — required for background playback and the alarm feature.

Fake Location

Fake Location lets you set a simulated GPS position on your own device for testing and privacy purposes.

Location data. The app reads your device's location so it can centre the map where you are and so the "Current GPS" button can pick up your real position. This location is used on the device only. It is never sent to us and never stored anywhere outside your phone.

Places you save. Names, addresses and coordinates you save are stored in a database inside the app's private storage on your device. They are not backed up to our servers and we have no access to them. You can delete any entry in the app, export them to a JSON file of your choosing, or remove them all by uninstalling the app.

Simulated location. While a simulated location is running, the app reports the coordinates you chose to Android as a mock position, so other apps on your device may read that position instead of your real one. This works only while you have selected Fake Location under Developer options, and only until you stop it. Nothing about this is transmitted to us.

Additional services used by this app:

  • Google Maps SDK for Android — draws the map. Google receives the map area you are viewing in order to serve map tiles.
  • Google Play services location — supplies the device position to the app.
  • Android Geocoder — converts an address you type into coordinates, and coordinates into an address label. On devices with Google services these lookups are handled by Google.

Permissions: location (precise and approximate) to show where you are on the map; mock location to report the simulated position, which Android allows only after you select the app under Developer options; foreground service and notifications to keep the simulated location running and show an ongoing notification while it is active.

Advertising consent in Europe

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, our apps ask for your consent before showing personalised advertising, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can choose to consent, to refuse, or to manage individual purposes.

You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from the privacy settings inside each app — the shield icon in Fake Location and World Radio, and the "Privacy settings" button in TV DSP Center. If you refuse, you will still see advertising, but it will not be personalised.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data deletion

Analytics data is anonymous and cannot be linked back to you. Uninstalling the app stops all data collection. You can also reset your advertising ID in your device's Google settings at any time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
