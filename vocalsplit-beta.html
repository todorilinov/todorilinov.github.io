Vocal Split · Beta programme

Coming soon. Help us test it first.

Vocal Split — the AI vocal remover, karaoke video maker and karaoke party for Windows — is almost ready. Before it goes on sale, a small group of people who work with karaoke and audio every day will put it through its paces.

Where things stand

  1. Internal testing — every feature is being tested on our own machines.
  2. Now: waiting for a certified code signature. The installer will be signed, so Windows knows who published it and SmartScreen does not warn about an unknown app. We do not hand out unsigned builds.
  3. Beta — accepted testers receive the signed installer and a Pro Edition beta key that unlocks everything for the test period.
  4. Release — with the bugs you found fixed.
