Inside the app
01 · The map

Drop a pin. That's your new position.

The whole app is one screen. Move the map, tap where you want to be, and the pin lands there. When you press ▶ Start FAKE Location, Android starts reporting those coordinates to the apps on your phone, and an ongoing notification reminds you it is running.

Need to get back to reality? Current GPS snaps the map to where you actually are, and stopping the simulation is the same single button.

  • Tap anywhere on the map to place the simulated position
  • Pinch, zoom and pan with the normal Google Maps controls
  • One button starts and stops the whole thing
02 · Controls

Six buttons, nothing hidden

Three in the top bar, three at the bottom. That is the entire interface.

Top bar

Search

Type an address, a city or a place name and the map jumps straight to it — no scrolling across the globe.

Top bar

Saved places

Your bookmarked locations in one list. Tap one and the pin is there instantly — ideal for the handful of spots you use again and again.

Top bar

Settings

Import and export your places as JSON, open the privacy settings and the privacy policy, share or rate the app.

Bottom

Current GPS

Centres the map on your real position — a quick way to start from where you are, or to check what the device actually sees.

Bottom

Save

Stores the pin you are on as a named place, so it joins your saved list and is one tap away next time.

Bottom

Start FAKE Location

Turns the simulation on. A foreground service keeps it alive with a permanent notification, and the same button stops it.

03 · Settings

Your places, in a file you own

Export JSON writes every saved place into a plain text file you can keep, back up or move to another phone. Import JSON reads it back. No account, no cloud, no sync server in the middle — the file goes wherever you put it.

The same screen holds Privacy settings, where you can change or withdraw your advertising consent at any time, and a direct link to the privacy policy.

Setup in 3 steps

Android will not let any app report a simulated position until you pick it yourself under Developer options. It is a one-time setup and it takes about a minute.

Step 1

Unlock Developer options

Open Settings → About phone → Software information and tap Build number seven times. Your device will ask for your PIN and then tell you that developer mode is on.

Step 2

Find the new menu

Go back to the main Settings list. A new Developer options entry now sits at the bottom, usually right under About phone.

Step 3

Select the mock location app

Inside Developer options scroll to Location → Select mock location app and choose Fake Location. That is it — the app can now simulate a position.

Worth knowing

  • The simulated position only affects your own device, and only while the simulation is running. Stop it, or clear the mock location app, and your phone is back to normal.
  • Some apps — banking, ride-hailing, a few games — deliberately detect mock locations and may refuse to work or restrict your account. Fake Location does not try to hide anything from them.
  • Nothing is sent to us. Your coordinates and saved places stay on the device; see the privacy policy for the details.
  • Use it for what it is good at: testing location features in your own apps, checking geofences, taking screenshots of another city, or simply not handing out your real position.
